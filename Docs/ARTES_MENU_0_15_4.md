# Artes de menu 0.15.4

Duas ilustrações originais geradas pela ferramenta integrada image_gen; sem login em site, sem API key. Não são modelos 3D nem mudanças nos mapas jogáveis. O texto e os botões são elementos funcionais da Unity sobrepostos às imagens, não texto pintado na arte.

Arquivos usados no projeto:
- Assets/ForjaDasMares/Resources/MenuArt/RoutesBackdrop.png — fundo do início/campanha/carregamento.
- Assets/ForjaDasMares/Resources/MenuArt/CampaignMap.png — ilustração do mapa de seleção.

Importação: textura sRGB, máximo2048, bilinear, sem mipmaps/alpha/leitura CPU, compressão HQ. MenuArtwork recorta mantendo proporção e nunca intercepta cliques. MenuArtImporter limita sua atuação à pasta MenuArt.

## Prompt 1 (fundo)
Use case: stylized-concept. Create an ORIGINAL high quality landscape background illustration for a stylized 3D medieval cooperative action RPG called Forja das Marés. This is a production game menu background, no UI, no lettering, no watermark. Wide cinematic panoramic composition: ancient rugged stone causeway winding from foreground into a vast underground ruin, mossy catacomb arches, a lush subterranean forest with soft turquoise bioluminescence in the middle distance, distant volcanic forge citadel glowing warm amber through haze on the far right. Painterly polished game key art, appealing chunky shapes, readable silhouettes, deep midnight teal and weathered bronze, warm torchlight, adventurous mysterious atmosphere, refined lighting, smooth clean edges, not pixel art. Keep the leftmost 40 percent dark and low contrast with subtle stone/foliage for readable menu text over it; place environmental focal points middle/right. No people, no existing franchise imagery, no logos. Landscape 16:9 or wider, high resolution.

## Prompt 2 (campanha)
Use case: stylized-concept. Production background artwork for an ORIGINAL medieval fantasy cooperative action RPG campaign selection map. Wide 16:9 landscape. A beautifully painted isometric expedition map viewed from high above: connected ancient catacomb ruins on the lower left, luminous turquoise underground forest at center, volcanic blackstone forge fortress on upper right, within a large cavern. One clearly readable winding stone road links the three regions across bridges. Rugged wild medieval style, miniature diorama feeling, polished stylized shapes, intricate tasteful environment details, teal moss, bone limestone, amber lava, restrained bronze accents. Atmospheric mist around the edges. Map occupies most of image; not a UI mockup. No labels, no text, no pins, no circles, no frames, no characters, no logo or watermark. Keep region centers sufficiently clear for overlaying three functional stage buttons later. Entire map readable and aesthetically coherent; smooth edges; not pixel art; original scene no franchise copies.
